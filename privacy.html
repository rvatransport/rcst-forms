Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

← Back to all forms

Last updated: July 17, 2026

River City Student Transport ("RCST," "we," "us," or "our") provides student and organization transportation services in the Richmond, Virginia area. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website, submit a transportation request, or receive trip communications from us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact information — parent/guardian or organization contact name, email address, mailing address, and phone number.
  • Student and trip information — student name, grade, school, pickup/drop-off hubs, schedule, and transportation plan.
  • Organization information — organization name, contact person, event details, and trip logistics.
  • Communications — messages you send us and your replies to our trip notifications.

We may also collect limited technical data (such as approximate location shared by a driver's device during an active trip) solely to provide live trip-status updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and respond to transportation requests.
  • Coordinate and deliver transportation services.
  • Send trip-status updates, arrival/departure notifications, and service alerts by email and, with your consent, by SMS text message.
  • Respond to questions and provide customer support.
  • Meet legal, safety, and billing obligations.

3. SMS / Text Message Program

Consent: By providing your mobile phone number and checking the SMS consent box on our request form, you expressly consent to receive transactional text messages from River City Student Transport related to your transportation service (trip-status updates, driver arrival/departure notices, delay and service alerts, and replies to your inquiries).

Message frequency varies based on your trip activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out: You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will send a confirmation message and then stop sending SMS messages. You may re-subscribe by submitting a new request or replying START.

Help: Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at info@rvatransport.com or (804) 840-8941.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, other than aggregators and service providers (such as our SMS delivery provider) that help us operate the messaging program.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to provide our services, including with:

  • Service providers that operate on our behalf, such as our email delivery provider (Resend), our SMS delivery provider (Twilio), and our form/hosting providers. These providers are contractually limited to using your information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety authorities when required by law or to protect the safety of students and the public.

Mobile opt-in and SMS consent data are never shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and to comply with our legal, accounting, and safety obligations. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data against unauthorized access.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services involve transportation of students, and the information we collect about students is provided by a parent, guardian, or authorized organization. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children without appropriate authorization.

8. Your Choices

  • You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You may unsubscribe from non-essential emails using the link in those emails.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
